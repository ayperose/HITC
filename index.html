Move beyond traditional SMS. Campus Connect sends parents and guardians real-time notifications from school: the actual time in and time out, announcements and advisories, school fee payments, eCash loads, grades and the student's Statement of Account.

Ask about Campus Connect

Driving innovation across the Philippines

HITC, Inc. (Headstart Information Technology & Consultancy Inc.) is one of the Philippines' leading IT firms, headquartered in Tuguegarao City, Cagayan.

We specialize in developing highly diversified automation products for schools, government agencies, and private institutions nationwide, staffed by young, creative, and dedicated professionals.

And we print books. Printing is a big part of what HITC does, and our team takes the books to schools to go through them together.
