What eBroadcasting does

One message, written once, reaches the people it is meant for: a grade level, a department or every parent.

Instant broadcast
Send school-wide messages in seconds: class cancellations, emergency alerts, event reminders and more.
Targeted audiences
Send to specific groups, such as a single grade level, a department or all parents, without messaging the whole school every time.

only who needs it

Scheduled broadcasts
Compose announcements in advance and schedule them to go out at the right time automatically.
SMS or Campus Connect
Announcements arrive by SMS on any mobile phone, with no app needed. Parents with the Campus Connect app get them there too, with clearer context and links they can tap.
Broadcast archive
Every past announcement is stored and searchable, so you never lose track of what was sent and when.
