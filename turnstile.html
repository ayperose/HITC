What the Turnstile System does

Students and staff tap their school ID to pass. The gate keeps the log, so nobody at the gate keeps a paper list.

RFID ID card entry
Students and staff tap their school ID to pass through. No manual logging: every entry and exit is recorded instantly.
Time-stamped logs
Every gate event is logged with a precise timestamp. Review the full entry and exit history of any student or staff member.
Parent alerts, by SMS or app
Parents get an automatic alert when their child enters or exits the campus: by SMS, with no app needed, or in the Campus Connect app.
Multi-gate support
Deploy turnstile units at different campus entrances, all managed from a single admin panel.
Attendance integration
Gate entry data feeds straight into the Attendance Manager, so nothing is entered twice.
