Technical Support Specialist

We're looking for a hands-on IT professional to support our growing network of school clients across Cagayan Valley. You'll be the first line of technical support: installing systems, troubleshooting issues and making sure our products run smoothly at every school we serve.

the role

Key responsibilities

  • Provide technical support for hardware and software issues at client schools
  • Troubleshoot network and system problems to keep downtime low
  • Install and maintain computer systems and HITC product deployments
  • Document and resolve technical issues in a timely and thorough manner
who we need

Qualifications

  • Graduate of IT, Computer Science or a related field
  • Experience in technical support or a similar field is an advantage
  • Solid knowledge of Windows and networking fundamentals
  • Strong problem-solving and analytical skills

Preferred skills

Nice to have for this role, on top of the qualifications above.

System maintenance
Experience maintaining and tuning computer systems for reliable day-to-day operation.
Network configuration
Hands-on experience setting up local area networks and Wi-Fi.
Hardware and software
Diagnosing and fixing both hardware failures and software configuration issues.
Routers and switches
Familiarity with configuring managed switches and routers in school networks.
Issue documentation
Logging technical incidents clearly and keeping service records for follow-up and reporting.
Client communication
Explaining technical things clearly to school administrators and staff who aren't technical.
