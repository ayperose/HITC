The Program

Build. Program.
Compete. Learn.

Intelliteach delivers a complete LEGO® MINDSTORMS® EV3 robotics curriculum to Philippine schools. Students learn engineering thinking through a hands-on build-program-test methodology, fully aligned to DepEd STEM standards.

Top students gain access to regional and national robotics competition preparation — producing the next generation of Filipino engineers and problem-solvers.
