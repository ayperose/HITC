What the Attendance Manager does

Every school day, on the record, without a paper register: the gate taps fill it in, and the reports build themselves.

Automated time in and time out
Students tap their ID at the Turnstile System to log their time in and time out automatically. No manual entry, no paper registers.

straight from the gate

Instant alerts to parents
Parents hear the moment their child taps in or out: by SMS, with no app needed on the parent's side, or in the Campus Connect app.
Attendance analytics
View daily, weekly and term-wide reports, and flag students with chronic absenteeism automatically.
Admin dashboard
School administrators get a real-time overview across all classes and grade levels from one screen.
Seamless integration
Works alongside SFMS and iAsk, so student records sync across the HITC ecosystem.
Mobile app for online classes
When classes move online, the Attendance Manager app takes over: students tap their virtual ID on their Android phone to mark attendance.
