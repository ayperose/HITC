What SFMS does

From the day a student enrolls to the day's last payment at the cashier, the school's money and records live in one system.

Tuition and fee management
Track tuition payments, installment plans and outstanding balances per student, and send automated reminders for overdue accounts.
Student enrollment
Manage enrollment records and admission data, from new enrollees to returning students, in one system.
Assessment and student ledger
Assess fees per student and keep an account ledger for each one: every charge, payment and balance tracked automatically.

every peso, per student

Billing, cashiering and accounting
Process billing, run the cashier and generate financial reports: a complete accounting solution for school finance departments.
eCash integration
Works with the HITC eCash System, so cashless transactions flow straight into SFMS records.
Audit trail
Every transaction is logged with the user and a timestamp, for full transparency and accountability.

where the payment lands

Fees, statements and the day's collection.

SFMS works with Campus Connect, HITC's app for parents and guardians. Parents get school fee payment notices and the student's Statement of Account. The Finance Officer gets the Daily Collection Report at the end of each day.
