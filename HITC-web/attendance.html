What Attendance Manager Does

Digital Roll Call

Replace paper registers with a fast mobile interface. Mark attendance by class, section, or subject in seconds.

Instant Parent Notifications

Automatically alert parents via SMS or push notification when their child is absent or late.

Attendance Analytics

View daily, weekly, and term-wide reports. Flag students with chronic absenteeism automatically.

Admin Dashboard

School administrators get a real-time overview across all classes and grade levels from one screen.

Seamless Integration

Works alongside SFMS and iAsk — student records sync across the entire HITC ecosystem.

Offline Mode

Take attendance even without internet. Data syncs automatically when connectivity is restored.
