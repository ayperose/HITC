How we got here

  1. 2006: Founded

    HITC is established in Tuguegarao City, Cagayan, with a focus on affordable IT consultancy for schools.

  2. 2007: Attendance and canteens

    For partner schools in the NCR and Region 1, we automated student attendance tracking and made canteen transactions cashless.

  3. 2010: Enrollment to billing

    For partner schools in the NCR, we brought enrollment, fee assessment, student accounts, billing and accounting into one platform.

  4. Today: Expanding nationwide

    Serving schools nationwide and expanding across the Philippines, with Campus Connect, our new system, sending parents real-time alerts from school.
