We believe every Filipino student deserves access to world-class educational technology. HITC develops cost-effective, locally built solutions that work for large campuses and small community schools alike.

Our team provides end-to-end support, from installation and teacher training to ongoing maintenance, so every school gets the most from its investment.

How we got here

  1. 20062006

    2006: Founded

    HITC is established in Tuguegarao City, Cagayan, with a focus on affordable IT consultancy for schools.

  2. 20072007

    2007: Attendance and eCash

    We developed and implemented an Attendance Monitoring and eCash Canteen System for partner schools in the NCR and Region 1, automating student attendance tracking and cashless canteen transactions.

  3. 20102010

    2010: School management platform

    We launched the Enrollment, Assessment, Student Ledger, Billing and Accounting System for partner schools in the NCR: one platform for enrollment, fee assessment, account monitoring and billing.

  4. TodayToday

    Today: Expanding nationwide

    Seven integrated product systems, serving schools nationwide and expanding across the Philippines.
