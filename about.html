Our Journey

How We Got Here

2006
Founded

HITC established in Tuguegarao City, Cagayan — focused on affordable IT consultancy for schools.

2007
Attendance & eCash

Developed and implemented an Attendance Monitoring and eCash Canteen System for partner schools in the NCR and Region 1, automating student attendance tracking and cashless canteen transactions.

2010
School Management Platform

Launched the Enrollment, Assessment, Student Ledger, Billing, and Accounting System for partner schools in the NCR — providing an integrated platform for student enrollment, fee assessment, account monitoring, and billing.

2013
EV3 Robotics

Introduced Intelliteach — bringing LEGO® MINDSTORMS® EV3 robotics to Philippine classrooms.

Today
Expanding Nationwide

7 integrated product systems serving schools nationwide and expanding across the Philippines.
