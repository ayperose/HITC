What loads, and from where

Most of the site comes from its own server. These are the only outside services it uses, and what each one can see.

Fonts, on every page
The site's typefaces are served from this website itself, so showing them sends nothing to Google or any other font service.
Google Maps, on Contact
The map on the Contact page is embedded from Google Maps and loads as you scroll near it. Google may set cookies and collect data under its own privacy policy. The directions links open Google Maps itself.
The message forms
The forms on the home page and the Contact page send nothing through this website. Pressing Send opens your own email app with your message written out to info@hitc.ph, and nothing goes until you send it from there. HITC then gets it as a normal email: your name, email address, school, the topic you picked and your message.
No analytics, no tracking
The site runs no analytics, ads or tracking pixels, sets no cookies of its own, and keeps nothing in your browser's storage.
The server
The service that hosts these pages may keep short technical logs, such as IP addresses and the pages asked for, to deliver the site and keep it safe.

Your rights under the Data Privacy Act

The Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) protects personal information in the Philippines. Under it, you can ask HITC what personal information it holds about you, and ask for it to be corrected or deleted. Write to info@hitc.ph.

This note covers this website only, not HITC's school systems or the Campus Connect app.
