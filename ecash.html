What the eCash System does

Lunch money becomes a wallet on the student's ID. Parents fill it, students tap it, and the school sees every peso.

Student digital wallet
Each student has a digital wallet loaded by parents. Funds are spent by tapping an RFID ID at any campus terminal.
Canteen, books and campus fees
Pay for canteen meals, books, school fees, photocopying and printing. Any campus payment is accepted at eCash terminals.

no cash needed

Parent top-up
Parents load their child's wallet online or through the mobile app at any time, so there is no need to send cash to school.
Spending alerts
Parents receive instant notifications for every transaction, so they can see how funds are spent at school.
SFMS and iAsk integration
All transactions sync with SFMS for school accounting. Balances and transaction history are also in the iAsk mobile app, for students and parents to check any time.
Transaction reports
Detailed daily and monthly reports for school administrators, showing revenue by category and department.
